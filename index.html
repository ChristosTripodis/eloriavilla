The villa

Quiet architecture among the olive trees of western Crete.

Eloria is taking shape on a 1,000 m² plot at the edge of Vamos, one of the oldest villages of Apokoronas. The village's stone lanes, tavernas and kafeneia are a short walk away, and from the garden the view opens towards the peaks of the Lefka Ori, the White Mountains.

The architecture is deliberately calm: white plastered volumes, tall arches, walls of local stone and timber pergolas. Inside, pale stone-look floors, warm walnut joinery and simple brushed-steel fittings.

Swim in the morning, walk to the village for coffee, and gather around the outdoor kitchen as the light softens over the hills.

Stone, white light and olive wood, in the green hills of Apokoronas.

Vamos, Apokoronas
